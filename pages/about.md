# Lena Savchenko

"Beauty is Truth - Truth Beauty, - that is all Ye know on earth, and all ye need to know," John Keats

This quote by John Keats says everything about me. Romantic, original and sincere and with idealistic views and lost connection to reality. I live in my own make believe world, as may of us do. 

I am fond of beautiful things, they seem to be perfect, absolutely complete, like the portrait of a lady I saw at the gallery this morning. It managed to caprure the ephemeral beauty of that lady and made it eternal, is not it wonderful? It is, it is wonderful in the sense of being unreal, unbelievable. Astonoshing!
